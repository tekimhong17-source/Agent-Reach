Questions people ask first

What happens if I forget my passphrase?
Your stored cards become permanently unreadable. There is no reset link and no support route around it — nobody holds a copy of your key. Write the passphrase down somewhere physical before you rely on the vault.
Do you ever see my card number?
No. Encryption happens in your browser before the request is sent. The server receives ciphertext plus the brand and last four digits for display, and it rejects anything that looks like a full card number as a safety net.
Does the camera upload my card photo?
No. The frame is read on your device and discarded. Nothing about the scan is transmitted — the only thing that ever leaves is the encrypted result.
What if I cancel?
Your existing cards stay encrypted and readable. You simply can't add beyond the free limit again. Cancelling never deletes your data.
Which devices work?
Any modern browser with a camera — iPhone, Android, laptop. Scanning needs an HTTPS connection, which the app always uses.

Stop keeping card numbers in your notes app

That's where most people keep them, and it's one stolen phone away from disaster. Give them a real home instead.

How the encryption works

When you save a card, your browser derives a 256-bit key from your passphrase using PBKDF2-SHA256 with 310,000 iterations and a fresh random salt for every card.

That key encrypts the card number, expiry and holder name with AES-256-GCM, using a fresh random initialisation vector. Only the resulting ciphertext, the salt and the IV are uploaded — along with the brand and last four digits so the vault list is readable at a glance.

The passphrase itself is never transmitted, never stored, and never derivable from what we hold. That is why we cannot reset it for you.
